## Goal

Fill the remaining `onLog` hooks so the quest log records combat, not just
event prose.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A monster activation logs its text, master actions and move (`ActivateDialogMoM.cs:35,80,116`)
- [ ] An attack logs its text (`InvestigatorAttack.cs:69`)
- [ ] An evade logs its text (`InvestigatorEvade.cs:49`)
- [ ] A horror check logs its text (`HorrorCheck.cs:65`)
- [ ] Newlines escaped on the way in, as the other entries are
- [ ] Tests that fail if any one of them stops being written

## Notes

`play.ts` passes `onLog: () => {}` to both `monsterDialog` and
`activationDialog` — the hooks are there and do nothing. Event text is logged
as of the phase-bar work; this is the rest of what a player would expect to
read back.
